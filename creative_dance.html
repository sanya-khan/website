Dance Education

Pre-Professional Training in Western Dance Forms
2017 – 2022

Pursued intensive multidisciplinary training in classical ballet, jazz, contemporary, Horton, hip-hop, tap, and musical theatre through ICMD's goal-oriented dance program, emphasizing technical development, mentorship, and preparation for internationally recognized Royal Academy of Dance (London) examinations. Classical Bharatanatyam Training
2011 – 2017

Completed all seven graded levels of Bharatanatyam study with consistent A+ performance, culminating in the Kovida certification. The program involved sustained training in technique, abhinaya, rhythm, theory, and stage performance — providing the classical rigor that later undergirded a movement vocabulary across multiple dance traditions.
