About Me

On curiosity,
people,
and craft.

UCLA undergraduate in Psychology & Dance — drawn to the mechanics of human behavior, the emotional logic behind brand and culture, and the art of translating insight into something that endures.

Foundation

Intellectual
Origins

I am a UCLA undergraduate pursuing a double major in Psychology and Dance, with a longstanding penchant for understanding the subtler mechanics of human behavior. Much of my intellectual life has been shaped by a curiosity about attention, trust, perception, and the conditions under which an experience acquires enough salience to endure.

"That curiosity has developed into a more defined interest in consumer psychology, behavioral insight, and the emotional logic that undergirds how people move through brands, environments, and different forms of communication."

Drive

Systems &
Purpose

My work has unfolded across research labs, editorial spaces, student leadership, and startup environments, but what undergirds this is a desire to build systems that are timeless and create lasting impact. I am intrigued by the systems that begin with appraisal and crystallise those insights into something tangible in the world.

Whether it becomes a strategy, an experience, a narrative, or a brand presence, what matters to me is making it more resonant through a real understanding of people. I care deeply about the confluence of psychological insight and lived experience. The most meaningful work feels to me like the idyll of intellectual pursuit — a form of seva (service) that serves not only the mind, but the world itself.

Practice

How I
Work

In previous roles, I have learned to move fluidly between analysis and execution. I have supported behavioral and social perception research, managed participant systems and data workflows, built sponsorship proposals, led outreach, and helped shape community-based events from early ideation to final delivery.

Taken together, these experiences have taught me how to read people carefully, organize ideas with clarity, and translate insight into something tangible and compelling. My background in dance has further opened me to the worlds of artists and creators, and with that, to a deeper sensitivity toward expression, emotions, and aesthetic instinct.

Horizon

Long-term
Vision

In the long term, I hope to build a career in product, brand, or consumer strategy, where behavioral insight can be translated into enduring human and commercial value. I pursue endeavors that are intellectually exacting and deeply alive to the complexities of the human psyche.

"I eventually hope to pursue an MBA and grow into leadership roles where discernment, imagination, and prowess can become a form of service to the world."
